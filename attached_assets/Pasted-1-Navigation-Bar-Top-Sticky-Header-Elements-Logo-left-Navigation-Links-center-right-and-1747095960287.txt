1. Navigation Bar (Top Sticky Header)
	•	Elements: Logo (left), Navigation Links (center/right), and a standout CTA button (far right)
	•	Goal: Easy access to different sections, persistent CTA for conversions
	•	Best Practice: Keep the CTA visually distinct (e.g., different color)

⸻

2. Hero Section
	•	Headline: Clear, bold statement of value or offer (e.g., “High Converting Heading Comes Here”)
	•	Subheadline: 1–2 lines of supporting info
	•	Buttons: Primary CTA (e.g., “Get Started”) and Secondary CTA (e.g., “Learn More”)
	•	Image/Video Placeholder: Use to reinforce message visually
	•	Trust Bar: Add logos of companies you’ve worked with to build credibility

⸻

3. Services Section
	•	Layout: 2 or 4 service blocks with:
	•	Title
	•	Short description
	•	CTA (e.g., “Learn More” or “Get Started”)
	•	Goal: Clearly state your offerings and allow quick access to each

⸻

4. Process Section
	•	Steps Format: Visual layout with icons or numbered steps (3–4 steps)
	•	Title and Brief Description per step
	•	Optional Visual: Photo of your team, workspace, or behind-the-scenes
	•	Purpose: Show transparency and ease of working with you

⸻

5. About Company Section
	•	Content:
	•	Short paragraph about your mission/vision
	•	Key metrics or stats (e.g., clients served, satisfaction rate, revenue growth)
	•	Optional Image: Team photo or company-related visual
	•	Purpose: Humanize your brand and share what makes you legit

⸻

6. Testimonial Section
	•	Layout: 3 cards or video-style testimonials with:
	•	Client photo (optional)
	•	Quote
	•	Client name + company/title
	•	Goal: Leverage social proof to drive trust and conversions

⸻

7. “Why Choose Us” Comparison Table
	•	Two Columns:
	•	What Others Provide (Generic Competitor Offerings)
	•	What We Provide (Your Unique Value Props)
	•	Highlight differences clearly with icons or standout text
	•	Goal: Show your differentiator at a glance

⸻

8. FAQ Section
	•	Accordion Format: Clickable dropdowns for each question
	•	Example Questions:
	•	“What is your refund policy?”
	•	“How does onboarding work?”
	•	“Is there a free trial?”
	•	Goal: Preempt objections and reduce hesitation

⸻

9. Final CTA Section
	•	Headline: Reassuring call to action (e.g., “Ready to get started?”)
	•	Subtext: Short reassurance or benefit
	•	Button: One final, bold CTA
	•	Optional Image/Visual: Reinforce the offer visually

⸻

10. Footer
	•	Links:
	•	Navigation (Pages)
	•	Legal (Privacy Policy, Terms)
	•	Social Media Icons
	•	Goal: Provide key info and reinforce brand trust at the bottom

⸻

Design Notes for Replit Implementation
	•	Mobile Responsive: Ensure each section adapts smoothly to mobile/tablet
	•	Styling: Clean, modern layout with a consistent color palette and font hierarchy
